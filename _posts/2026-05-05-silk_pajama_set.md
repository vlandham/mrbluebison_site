Every great garment has a backstory. This one has a second act.

It begins in Bangkok, with a silk shawl made to coordinate with a matching custom-made dress. A perfect pair, destined for glamorous evenings. Unfortunately, as perfect pairs sometimes do, they never really went out together.

Enter Bethany, who believes no fabric should go unworn. Armed with the [Fifi Pyjamas pattern](https://tillyandthebuttons.com/products/fifi-pyjamas-sewing-pattern) from Tilly and the Buttons, she gave that shawl a remix: a halter-tie cami and matching shorts, the printed silk paneled against cream silk rescued from the scrap bin.

To get technical: B cut a size 2. The Fifi shorts didn't quite fit the way she wanted, so she swapped in the shorts from the [Carolyn Pajamas](https://closetcorepatterns.com/products/carolyn-pajamas-pattern) by Closet Core Patterns. One part Tilly, one part Closet Core, all parts luxurious.

From a shawl that never got its night out to pajamas made for nights in.
