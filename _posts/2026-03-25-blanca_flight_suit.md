Ground control to Major B: your flight suit is ready.

The Blanca Flight Suit from [Closet Core Patterns](https://closetcorepatterns.com/products/blanca-flight-suit-pattern) is something of a fan favorite, and this one is easily the most complimented of all Bethany's creations. Friends ask where she bought it. The answer, of course, is that she didn't.

Sewn in stretch denim, it strikes the perfect balance of comfort and class, which has made it her go-to garment for indie concert going. Nothing says "I liked this band before you did" quite like a perfectly fitted coverall.

To get technical for a second: Bethany cut a size 4 and graded to a 6 in the waist. Perfection!
